Rules & display

Outer walls are drawn inside the building outline, so the floor area is the space you can actually use. Inner walls between rooms are drawn 12 cm thick.

The dashed red line shows the minimum distance from buildings to the plot border. In Lithuania 3 m is the usual minimum without your neighbour's consent, but check your detailed plan. Coverage (užstatymo tankumas) is calculated from the official area.

Save & share

The plan is saved in this browser automatically. To show it to someone, send them a share link.

  • ←→↑↓ nudge 10 cm, with Shift 1 m
  • R rotate 90°, D duplicate, Del delete
  • Shift while rotating turns off snapping
  • Double-click a wall handle to add corners, a corner to remove it
  • Right-click (or double-click) a house to plan its rooms
